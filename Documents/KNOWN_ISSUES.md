# Known Issues and Constraints

- USB requires Android Developer Options and USB debugging; enterprise policies may prohibit it.
- Virtual microphone is not part of v1; OBS receives streamed audio through the native plugin.
- Virtual-camera acceptance varies by third-party application and must be validated per release.
- Wi-Fi is same-LAN/private-network only; guest Wi-Fi, AP isolation, and multicast-blocking networks may require manual IP or fail.
- Manual camera controls and 60 FPS depend on camera hardware and Android vendor implementation.
- 4K, HDR, recording, screen sharing, remote access, multi-phone production, and non-Windows platforms are deferred.
